Help Center

Frequently asked questions and guides to help you get started.

How does Scriptly AI generate scripts?

Scriptly uses the advanced Gemini AI models to analyze your topic, target length, and structural requirements. It then structures a high-retention video script complete with hooks, visual cues, and outros.

Can I use the scripts for commercial videos?

Yes, absolutely! All scripts generated by Scriptly AI belong to you. You can use them for monetized YouTube channels, client work, ads, or any other commercial purpose without attribution.

What format is the output, and how do I copy it?

Scriptly structures scripts into distinct blocks (Title, Hook, Body, and Outro). You can click the "Copy Script" button to instantly format and copy the entire text to your clipboard.

Is there a limit on script length?

We offer three preset lengths: Short (1-2 min), Medium (5-8 min), and Long (10-15 min). These translate to target word ranges tailored for typical video pacing.
